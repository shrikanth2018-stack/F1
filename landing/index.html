How it works

Home cooking, four taps away

No middlemen. No factory food. Just home cooking, prepared fresh each day and hand-delivered warm.

  1. 01

    Browse your meal cycle

    Open the app and pick a cycle — breakfast, lunch, snacks, or dinner. Each shows the day's fresh menu and its order cut-off.

  2. 02

    Order before the cut-off

    Add meals and essentials, then pay by UPI, card, or your 1stOne wallet. The app shows exactly when each item arrives — today or tomorrow.

  3. 03

    We cook it fresh

    After the cut-off, our team prepares your meal fresh in our home kitchen — sealed warm in food-grade packaging.

  4. 04

    Our staff will deliver

    Our delivery staff hand-delivers during the cycle window. Track every stage in the app and get a ping when it's on the way.

Subscriptions

Subscribe once, eat well every day

Choose a plan length that suits you and lock in your meals at the best price. It delivers automatically, every day — no daily ordering, no forgetting.

  • Pause anytime. Travelling or eating out? Pause with one tap and resume whenever you like.
  • Skip single days. Tap any date on the calendar to skip just that day.
  • Never lose a paid meal. Pauses and skips don't burn days — your plan simply runs longer until every meal you paid for is delivered.
  • Food or essentials. Subscribe to daily meals, daily essentials, or both.

Features

Order, pay, track, and earn — all in one place, built to make eating well effortless.

Order a one-off meal for today, or subscribe for daily delivery. Mix and match as you go.
